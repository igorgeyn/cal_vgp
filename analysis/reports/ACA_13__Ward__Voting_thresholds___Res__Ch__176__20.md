# ACA 13 (Ward) — Protect and Retain the Majority Vote Act

## What It Does

ACA 13 would amend the California Constitution to require that any ballot measure proposing to increase the voting threshold for future measures must itself pass by that same increased threshold. For example, if a measure seeks to require a two-thirds vote for future tax increases, that measure itself would need to pass by a two-thirds vote rather than a simple majority. (CalBallot existing summary)

## Why It's on the Ballot

This legislative constitutional amendment was authored by Assemblymember Chris Ward and placed on the ballot by the state legislature. It aims to prevent the use of simple majority votes to impose supermajority requirements on future ballot measures. (CalBallot existing summary)

## Arguments For

- The measure protects local control by preventing statewide voters from easily overriding local decision-making with artificially high voting requirements (CalBallot existing summary)
- It prevents abuse of the initiative process by ensuring that measures cannot impose harder voting thresholds on future measures than they themselves had to meet (CalBallot existing summary)
