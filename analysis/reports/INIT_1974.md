## What It Does

This ballot measure would establish a public campaign financing system in California, allowing eligible candidates to receive public funds for their campaigns instead of relying solely on private donations. The measure implements the California Fair Elections Act of 2026, which was enacted by the legislature in Chapter 245 of 2025. (CalBallot existing summary)
