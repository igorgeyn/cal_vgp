## What It Does

This ballot measure would establish a public campaign financing system in California, allowing eligible candidates to receive government funding for their campaigns instead of relying solely on private donations. The measure implements the California Fair Elections Act of 2026 as an amendment to the existing Political Reform Act of 1974. (CalBallot)

## Why It's on the Ballot

This measure was authored by Senator Tom Umberg and placed on the ballot through the legislative process. (CalBallot)
