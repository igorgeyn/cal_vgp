# California Proposition 50, Use of Legislative Congressional Redistricting Map Amendment (2025)

**ID:** PROP_50
**Year:** 2025
**Jurisdiction:** Statewide, California
**Type:** None
**Status:** Passed

---

## What It Does

This constitutional amendment would temporarily allow the California Legislature to draw congressional district maps for elections from 2025 through 2030, replacing maps that would normally be drawn by the independent Citizens Redistricting Commission. After 2030, the Citizens Redistricting Commission would resume drawing congressional district maps following each census (CalBallot summary).

## Why It's on the Ballot

This measure was placed on the ballot by the Legislature in response to Texas conducting mid-decade partisan congressional redistricting (CalBallot summary). It also establishes state policy supporting the use of fair, independent, and nonpartisan redistricting commissions nationwide (CalBallot summary).
