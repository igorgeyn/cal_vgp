AI-generated plain-language summary

This measure authorizes Pleasant View Elementary School District to issue $3,000,000 in bonds to acquire and improve school facilities, including multi-purpose areas for science, technology, math, music and arts. The bonds would be repaid through property tax levies of approximately $59 per $100,000 of assessed value, generating an average of $204,000 annually while the bonds remain outstanding.
