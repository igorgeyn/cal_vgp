City of Chino — Municipal Code Amendment

City of Chino, San Bernardino County • 2026 • Measure

Upcoming

CalBallot explanation · AI-assisted and source-checked

Change Chino’s business license tax. Larger businesses would pay $0.60 to $1.50 per $1,000 of gross receipts, depending on business category; businesses with $75,000 or less in gross receipts would pay $25. The question estimates $4.8 million annually until ended by voters. Revenue would enter the city’s General Fund for general services, with audits and spending disclosure.

Official source: https://elections.sbcounty.gov/elections/2026/1103/measures/

Approval requirement: Majority (county listing).

Official ballot question

Without taxing residents, and to support general city services such as 911/police response; protect local drinking water sources; retain/attract local businesses; shall the measure be adopted to reform City of Chino’s outdated business license ordinance with rates of $0.60 - $1.50 per $1,000 of gross receipts for larger businesses based on business category; and only $25 for smaller businesses (under $75,000), generating approximately $4,800,000 annually until ended by voters; with audits, spending disclosure, funds locally controlled?

Official ballot wording and filing (PDF page 3). Wording transcribed from the official filing; line breaks and spacing normalized.

  • The question says “under $75,000” for the smaller-business rate; Section 7 of the same resolution says “$75,000 or less.” The explanation follows Section 7.
