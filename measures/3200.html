Shall the City replace its 2018 Parcel Tax for the San Francisco Unified School District with a new tax that changes the annual tax rate from $320 per parcel to $288 per parcel, adjusted for inflation each year, and with an exemption for people age 65 or older, until June 30, 2038, for an estimated revenue of $48.1 million a year?

1, San Francisco County • 2020 • Measure

Passed

Result: 74.4% yes, 25.6% no (404,090 votes cast)

AI-generated plain-language summary

This measure replaced San Francisco's 2018 school parcel tax with a new tax that reduced the rate from $320 to $288 per parcel annually, added annual inflation adjustments and a senior exemption, and extended the tax until 2038. The new tax was projected to generate approximately $48.1 million per year for the San Francisco Unified School District.
