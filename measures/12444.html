Cabrillo Unified School District — Parcel Tax Measure

Cabrillo Unified School District, San Mateo County • 2026 • Measure

Upcoming

CalBallot explanation · AI-assisted and source-checked

Replace Cabrillo’s existing school parcel tax with a $239-per-parcel tax for five years, estimated to raise $2.6 million annually. Funds would support teachers, specialized educators, career training and college-readiness programs. The measure provides senior exemptions, prohibits use for administrator salaries or pensions, and requires public spending disclosure.

Official source: https://smcacre.gov/elections/november-3-2026-statewide-general-election

Approval requirement: Two-thirds (county listing).

Official ballot question

With funds that cannot be taken by the State or Federal Government and spent elsewhere, shall Cabrillo Unified School District’s measure to support local students by hiring and retaining highly qualified teachers and specialized educators; and expanding career training and college readiness programs, including agriculture, construction, and welding be adopted, replacing the current parcel tax at $239 per parcel for five years (raising $2,600,000 annually) with senior exemptions, no administrator salaries or pensions, and public disclosure of spending?
