The measure would have established a four-year special parcel tax of $36 annually for single-family homes (with specified amounts for other property types) to fund local police and fire services in Orange Cove, expected to raise approximately $98,000 per year. The measure required a two-thirds majority to pass but received only 59.6% approval and therefore failed.
