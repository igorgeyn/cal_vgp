In order to deliver street, park, sewer, and water improvements and other public works projects more efficiently and less expensively, shall Sections 400 and 1006 of the City Charter be amended to create more flexibility in the methods used to deliver those projects when the City Council designates, by ordinance, the methods allowed and determines that a particular project would benefit from those methods?

2, Ventura County • 2020 • Measure

Passed

Result: 73.6% yes, 26.4% no (57,220 votes cast)

AI-generated plain-language summary

The measure amended the City Charter to give the City Council more flexibility in choosing delivery methods for public works projects like street, park, sewer, and water improvements. The amendment allows the Council to designate alternative project delivery methods through ordinance when they determine a particular project would benefit from those methods.
