Shall the measure providing for an annual special tax of $600.00 on each parcel of real property for an unlimited duration be levied within the Mortara Circle Community Services District, which would be an increase to the current special tax of $350.00 per year, to be used only for road improvement and maintenance services and generating an annual revenue of $18,600.00, commencing fiscal year 2021/2022, be adopted?

1, El Dorado County • 2020 • Measure

Failed

Result: 23.3% yes, 76.7% no (60 votes cast)

AI-generated plain-language summary

The measure would have increased the annual special tax on each property parcel in the Mortara Circle Community Services District from $350 to $600 per year for road improvement and maintenance services. The tax increase would have generated approximately $18,600 annually starting in fiscal year 2021/2022 and would have continued indefinitely.
