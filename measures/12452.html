City of Burlingame — Transient Occupancy Tax Measure

City of Burlingame, San Mateo County • 2026 • Measure

Upcoming

CalBallot explanation · AI-assisted and source-checked

Increase Burlingame’s transient occupancy tax from 12% to 15%, paid by hotel and lodging guests. The question estimates $5.7 million annually until ended by voters for general city services, with independent citizen oversight and local control. Listed uses include streets, parks, emergency response and wildfire prevention.

Official source: https://smcacre.gov/elections/november-3-2026-statewide-general-election

Approval requirement: Majority (county listing).

Official ballot question

To support city services such as: maintaining city streets/sidewalks and repairing potholes; maintaining parks and trees; enhancing emergency response, preparedness, and wildfire prevention; and for general government use; shall the City of Burlingame measure be adopted raising the transient occupancy (hotel) tax from 12% to 15%, paid only by hotel and lodging guests, until ended by voters, providing an estimated $5,700,000 annually, all funds locally controlled, with independent citizen oversight?
