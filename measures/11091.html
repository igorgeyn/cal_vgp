AI-generated plain-language summary

This measure authorized the Riverdale Joint Unified School District to issue $25.9 million in bonds to fund facility improvements including expanding vocational education facilities, constructing permanent classrooms to replace portables, renovating classrooms and athletic facilities, and updating infrastructure systems. The bonds would be repaid through property tax levies estimated at less than 6 cents per $100 of assessed value through 2052.
